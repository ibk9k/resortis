# Price accommodation and leisure facilities separately

In Management play, accommodation and leisure facilities have separate prices, with nightly room payments, per-use facility fees, a fixed room rate for an existing booking, and normal reservation of guest funds for remaining nights. Reserved funds remain with the guest until payment. The accepted exception is rare carefree spenders who can spend those reserves on activities and leave when unable to afford another night, with their profile explaining the reason. Actual rates, nightly payment timing, and per-building versus per-type price setting remain open; Creative mode overrides are being designed separately.
