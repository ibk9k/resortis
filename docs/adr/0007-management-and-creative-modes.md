# Support Management and Creative play

The game supports standard Management play and Creative tools as product features. Creative can be used in any save, with a backup before the first change and the edited world retained on return to Management; it has a convenient preset and individual powers. The resort simulates normally by default, with needs/dirt/wear overrides available, and physical placement restrictions remain active. Day skipping processes the simulation, with visual time-of-day control separate. Money/progression/time controls and guest/needs/cleaning/repair tools appear in a visible panel with a shortcut. Disabling unlimited funds restores the remembered finite balance, including deliberate grants. Exact presets and detailed save/command behavior remain to be designed; see creative-mode.md.
