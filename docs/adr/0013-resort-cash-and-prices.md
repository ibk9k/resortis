# 0013 · Resort cash and prices

The starter resort is already owned and opens with $2,500. The top bar shows opening cash plus the net movement in the journal. Existing saves retain their recorded income and wages and inherit the same starting capital. They begin paying upkeep at the next day boundary. Starting capital appears separately from profit and loss.

Each catalogue item and stone path has a purchase cost and an upkeep schedule. A path costs $5 per tile. Landscaping, furniture and paths with zero upkeep list that rate in the catalogue schedule. Other upkeep is charged for each placed item present at the boundary after a 180-second resort day. Janitors remain free to hire and earn $30 per completed day. Operating expenses may take cash below zero. New construction requires enough cash to pay its price.

Valid construction records a purchase after placement and access checks succeed. Moving, rotation and bulldozing have no cash effect. Undoing an actual purchase records a full refund. Redo purchases it again and rechecks cash and placement. Refunds reduce construction expense. The statement treats construction as a cash expense and shows today, lifetime totals, and daily history. Receipts can be browsed in reverse posting order.

Manage → Operating results → Prices edits one whole-dollar price per active guest service type, from $1 to $999. Defaults are $60 per cabin night and $12, $8 and $10 per guest for meals, sunbeds and volleyball respectively. A booking keeps its reserved nightly rate. A visit uses the current activity price at physical admission. Volleyball checks affordability for both players before charging either. The pool and fishing pier have construction and upkeep schedules and will gain guest prices when their paid activities are implemented.

Prices and the financial journal save with the full resort. Historical receipts retain the price actually paid. Loading validates receipt identities, amounts, journal totals, and the live guest and staff records before replacing the open resort. The schedule and opening balance are initial playtest values.
