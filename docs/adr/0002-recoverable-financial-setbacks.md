# Make financial setbacks recoverable

Financial trouble will remain recoverable and will not force the player to lose the resort or restart through a bankruptcy game-over. Poor decisions should still affect income, expansion, and the need to change operations, preserving meaningful management while supporting the player's investment in building and decoration. Run-ending bankruptcy was considered and the user chose recoverable setbacks. The user's preferred recovery direction is a loan plus contextual guidance; the loan terms, any expansion restrictions, and the intensity of financial pressure remain open design decisions.
