# Build around one growing beachside resort

The initial main game will follow one persistent beachside resort, with waves along one map side and the main resort behind the shore. Adjacent inland and beach parcels become purchasable after resort milestones. Booked guests can stroll and relax on the beach freely; placed beach facilities charge per use. Beach-specific facilities and outdoor decorations can be placed on sand, while ordinary cabins and shops initially remain inland. Volleyball, sunbathing, and fishing areas are in the initial scope. Management aims for prestigious top-tier resort status, with additional side goals/missions and continued play afterward. Other locations remain a future possibility; exact milestones, missions, parcel sizes/prices, and the map side remain open.
