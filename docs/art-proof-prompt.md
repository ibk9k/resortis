# First concept-frame prompt

Intent: original visual planning concept, generated with the built-in image generation tool. The user's supplied screenshots guide the visual language. This is not an asset sheet or an engine capture.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept / game-view planning frame.

Create a wide 16:9 concept frame for an original isometric pixel-art beach resort management game. The visual target is the dense, charming early-2010s isometric browser-tycoon pixel aesthetic: crisp hand-placed-looking pixel clusters, diamond floor tiles, dark selective outlines, cutaway furnished rooms, and tiny chibi guests with large expressive heads. Maintain one consistent pixel density, an approximately 2:1 ground projection, and a fixed camera. Keep the world bright, detailed, and readable at a small size. This is pixel art, with sharply stepped edges and restrained dithering.

Scene: ocean along only the foreground-left diagonal edge, short white pixel-foam waves meeting warm sandy beach. Behind the beach, a compact developed resort extends inland toward the upper right. Include a sunbathing facility with striped parasols and loungers on sand; inland include several complete cutaway wooden cabins with beds and tidy furnishings, a reception hut, a compact food/drink counter, a small pool, stone paths, palms, flowers, hedges, and a furnished garden. Terrain eligibility reads clearly: cabins and shops inland, beach leisure on sand. Keep the composition like a believable playable resort layout with accessible paths, rather than a display of isolated objects.

People: a modest number of tiny expressive pixel guests strolling, relaxing, and enjoying the resort. Include a recognizable janitor tending a small mess and a maintenance worker near a facility. Their proportions match the buildings and furniture. Use warm wood and sand, lush saturated greens, turquoise water, and controlled colorful accents.

Interface: understated pixel-inspired frames/icons in muted dark green/charcoal with warm cream readable text. Include a narrow top money/day/mode HUD, a compact grouped building catalogue near the bottom, and one small contextual guest card on the right. Leave most of the screen for the resort. Keep text sparse: "MANAGEMENT", "DAY 3", "$12,450", "BUILD", "GUEST", "Mood", "Likes". Use clear text instead of decorative unreadable lettering.

Avoid photorealism, smooth vector illustration, painterly blur, 3D rendered materials, shallow depth of field, giant characters, excessive UI, neon green panel borders, logos, watermarks, or copied layouts/branding from an existing game. Create original assets and an original arrangement, while preserving the requested cozy isometric pixel-tycoon character.
