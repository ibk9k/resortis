# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows PC players building and managing an inviting beachside resort with mouse and keyboard. The project owner directs and playtests; the assistant handles implementation and the asset workflow.

## Product Purpose

Combine expressive resort building, guests enjoying their surroundings, and meaningful accommodation and facility pricing. Management should remain recoverable. Creative tools let players reshape the same resort freely.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, lively, detailed. The selected visual direction is the richer v1 concept, with v3's broader beach. The reference screenshots establish isometric cutaway buildings, expressive people, and dense, readable resort activity.

## Anti-references

The simpler v2 concept was not selected. Avoid reducing the world to generic flat shapes, making the beach a decorative sliver, or reproducing the original game's crowded green interface.

## Design Principles

- Let the resort occupy the screen; reveal tools when they help the current task.
- Make building placement, access, and guest reactions easy to understand.
- Give the shoreline enough usable room for the planned beach facilities.
- Keep visual richness consistent across terrain, buildings, plants, and people.
- Preserve the player's work when moving between Management and Creative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Agreed: clear scalable text, panning and zooming, a calmer interface, pause, and adjustable simulation speed. No specific accessibility certification has been requested. Implementation defaults should include keyboard alternatives, visible focus, readable contrast, and status explanations that do not rely on color alone.
