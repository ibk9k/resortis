# Resort management

The language of the resort-building and management game. These terms describe the game world; detailed mechanics are specified in the design documents as decisions are resolved.

## Language

**Management mode**:
The standard way of playing the resort, with its economy, guest simulation, operating rules, and milestone progression.
_Avoid_: Creative mode when referring to standard management play.

**Creative mode**:
A way of playing focused on unrestricted building resources and direct control over the resort's money, progression, and time.
_Avoid_: Management mode when referring to creative play.

**Creative tools**:
Player controls for directly changing resort resources, progression, time, or simulation state.
_Avoid_: Staff controls when referring to direct changes to the game world.

**Creative preset**:
A convenient predefined combination of Creative tool settings that can also be adjusted individually.
_Avoid_: Fixed ruleset when referring to the adjustable preset.

**Simulation time**:
The in-game time governing guest stays, activities, needs, and economic events.
_Avoid_: Visual time of day when referring to elapsed gameplay time.

**Visual time of day**:
The lighting state of the scene, which Creative tools can set independently of advancing the simulation.
_Avoid_: Day skip when referring only to changing the lighting.

**Day skip**:
Advancing the resort simulation through the elapsed day and its guest, staff, and economic events.
_Avoid_: Lighting change when referring to actual elapsed simulation.

**Resort**:
The property the player builds, decorates, and manages.
_Avoid_: Park, hotel when referring to the entire property.

**Beach**:
The sandy shoreline beside the main resort grounds, used by guests for strolling and relaxation.

**Land expansion**:
The extension of the resort grounds into additional land as the property develops.
_Avoid_: New location when referring to growth of the existing resort.

**Land parcel**:
A portion of land that can become available for purchase to expand the resort, including inland and beach portions.
_Avoid_: New location when referring to an addition to the existing resort.

**Resort milestone**:
An in-game resort goal whose completion makes additional content available to purchase.
_Avoid_: Development milestone when referring to an in-game progression goal.

**Side mission**:
An additional objective the player can pursue alongside the main Management goal.
_Avoid_: Mandatory milestone when referring to a side objective.

**Mission board**:
The collection of optional side goals and challenges available for the player to pursue.
_Avoid_: Milestone gate when referring to optional objectives.

**Remembered balance**:
The finite resort cash value retained while unlimited funds are active, including deliberate money grants, and restored when unlimited funds are disabled.
_Avoid_: Unlimited funds when referring to this numeric cash value.

**Unlock**:
Making a building or decoration available for purchase after its progression conditions are met.
_Avoid_: Purchase when referring to availability rather than acquiring the item.

**Guest**:
A simulated person who books accommodation for an overnight stay at the resort.
_Avoid_: Day visitor, NPC when referring to an overnight guest.

**Day visitor**:
A person who visits the resort without booking accommodation or staying overnight.
_Avoid_: Guest when specifically referring to a day-only visit.

**Stay**:
The period during which a guest uses the resort and its accommodation as an overnight customer.
_Avoid_: Day visit when referring to a stay.

**Planned stay length**:
The number of in-game nights a guest intends to stay at the resort.
_Avoid_: Actual stay length when referring to the intended duration.

**Actual stay length**:
The number of in-game nights a guest completes before checking out.
_Avoid_: Planned stay length when referring to the completed duration.

**Early checkout**:
A guest's departure before their planned stay ends.
_Avoid_: Bankruptcy when referring to an individual guest leaving early.

**Departure warning**:
A notice that a guest is considering early checkout because of prolonged severe unhappiness.
_Avoid_: Checkout confirmation when the guest has not yet decided to leave.

**Accommodation unit**:
A complete furnished room or cabin, providing basic bathroom and shower access, that the player places as a single unit for guest lodging.
_Avoid_: Furniture, room shell when referring to the complete accommodation unit.

**Accommodation selection**:
A guest's choice among available accommodation units based on price and suitability.
_Avoid_: Price setting when referring to a guest choosing a unit.

**Leisure facility**:
A resort attraction or service that guests can use for recreation or relaxation.
_Avoid_: Accommodation unit when referring to a recreational offering.

**Beach facility**:
A leisure facility designed for the sandy shoreline, such as a volleyball court, sunbathing area, or fishing area.
_Avoid_: Beach access when referring to a placed recreational offering.

**Facility price**:
The price charged to a guest for one use of a leisure facility, set separately from accommodation prices.
_Avoid_: Accommodation price, package price when referring to a facility's own price.

**Nightly payment**:
An accommodation payment covering one night of a guest's stay.
_Avoid_: Full-stay prepayment when referring to a nightly payment.

**Booking rate**:
The agreed nightly accommodation price for a guest's booked stay.
_Avoid_: Current advertised rate when referring to a price already agreed for a booking.

**Accommodation reserve**:
The portion of a guest's budget set aside for unpaid nights of the planned stay, remaining the guest's money until nightly payment.
_Avoid_: Revenue when referring to money that has not yet been paid to the resort.

**Carefree spender**:
A guest whose activity spending can use up their accommodation reserve, leading to early checkout if they cannot afford another night.
_Avoid_: Unhappy guest when referring specifically to a guest leaving for lack of funds.

**Building orientation**:
The facing direction selected for a placed building.
_Avoid_: Camera rotation when referring to a building's own orientation.

**Decoration**:
A furnishing or landscape element used to personalize the resort's appearance.
_Avoid_: Cosmetic when it implies decoration cannot affect the resort's performance.

**Local appeal**:
The attractiveness of a particular part of the resort, including the benefit that nearby decoration gives accommodation in that area.
_Avoid_: Resort appeal when referring to a specific area's attractiveness.

**Resort appeal**:
The attractiveness of the resort as a whole, to which decoration throughout the property contributes.
_Avoid_: Local appeal, guest satisfaction when referring to the overall attractiveness of the property.

**Price**:
The amount of in-game currency charged to a guest for a resort offering.
_Avoid_: Cost when referring to what a guest is charged rather than what the resort spends.

**Guest budget**:
The finite amount of in-game money a guest has available for accommodation and activities during a stay.
_Avoid_: Production budget when referring to a guest's spending money.

**Guest preference**:
A visible personal interest that influences a guest's choice of accommodation or activities.
_Avoid_: Need when referring to a guest's personal interest.

**Guest need**:
A recurring personal requirement that a guest seeks to satisfy during a stay.
_Avoid_: Preference when referring to an everyday requirement rather than an individual interest.

**Mood**:
A guest's current emotional response to their experience and surroundings.
_Avoid_: Resort appeal when referring to a guest's emotional state.

**Guest review**:
A departing guest's feedback on their stay, with reasons for their assessment.
_Avoid_: Departure reason when referring to an evaluation of the whole stay.

**Reputation**:
The resort's standing based on guest feedback, influencing future bookings.
_Avoid_: Resort appeal when referring to the resort's history of guest experiences rather than its attractiveness.

**Hygiene**:
A guest's personal cleanliness need.
_Avoid_: Area cleanliness when referring to the cleanliness of the guest themselves.

**Area cleanliness**:
The condition of a resort area with respect to dirt and mess.
_Avoid_: Hygiene when referring to the surrounding environment rather than a guest.

**Dirt**:
Mess in the resort that guests can encounter and janitors can remove.
_Avoid_: Decoration when referring to mess that needs cleaning.

**Janitor**:
A member of resort staff responsible for removing dirt and maintaining area cleanliness.
_Avoid_: Guest, maintenance worker when referring specifically to cleaning staff.

**Maintenance worker**:
A member of resort staff responsible for maintaining and repairing facilities.
_Avoid_: Janitor when referring to repair work rather than cleaning.

**Staff wage**:
The recurring daily expense of employing a resort staff member.
_Avoid_: Hiring cost when referring to ongoing wages.

**Building upkeep**:
The recurring daily expense of operating a resort building.
_Avoid_: Purchase cost when referring to ongoing building expenses.

**Work zone**:
An area designated by the player for a janitor's cleaning work.
_Avoid_: Patrol route when referring to an area rather than an ordered path.

**Cleanup task**:
The work of removing a particular mess at a location in the resort.
_Avoid_: Work zone when referring to an individual piece of cleaning work.

**Perceived value**:
A guest's assessment of whether a resort offering is worth its price.
_Avoid_: Affordability when referring to whether a guest considers a purchase worthwhile.

**Recovery**:
The return of a financially struggling resort to sustainable operation while retaining the player's resort.
_Avoid_: Restart, reset when referring to recovery.

**Recovery loan**:
Borrowed in-game funds intended to help a struggling resort restore its operations.
_Avoid_: Revenue when referring to borrowed funds.
